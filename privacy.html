Privacy Policy

LetsLog for iPhone · effective 1 December 2026

In one sentence: LetsLog stores everything on your iPhone, has no account and no server, and the developer cannot see your data.

Who we are

LetsLog is developed by Mark Bamford, Germany (the "developer"). Contact: mml.bamford@gmail.com.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Nothing to the developer. There is no LetsLog server, no account and no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit): when you add a property or the app works out a route, the address or coordinates are sent to Apple's Maps service, like any Maps app on your iPhone. Apple's handling of this is described in Apple's privacy policy.
  • App Store: subscriptions are handled by Apple. The developer receives no personal details from a purchase.
  • Exports you make: the PDF and CSV files go wherever you choose to share them (e.g. to your accountant). That is under your control.
  • iCloud backup: if you back up your iPhone to iCloud, your LetsLog data is included in that backup, which Apple encrypts, as with any app.

Location

LetsLog asks for location access set to "Always" so that visits are logged while the app is closed. It uses Apple's region monitoring for the addresses you add (and your home base) and Apple's visit monitoring, which reports places where the phone stayed. It does not keep a continuous track of your movements, and no location data is transmitted to the developer. You can change the permission at any time in iPhone Settings → LetsLog → Location.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt. Receipt text is read on the device with Apple's Vision framework. Photos you attach are copied into the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Your rights and control

  • Export all your records at any time (Report → Export).
  • Delete everything with one tap (Settings → Delete all data). Deleting the app also deletes its data.
  • Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on request — but you can still contact us with any question.

Children

LetsLog is intended for adults managing rental property and is not directed at children.
