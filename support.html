Common questions

A visit wasn't logged

Check iPhone Settings → LetsLog → Location is set to Always with Precise Location on. Visits under 5 minutes are ignored (adjustable in Settings). If you park some way from the property, make the arrival zone bigger in the property's details. The assistant on the Today tab will usually offer to add a visit your phone saw but the app missed.

The app logged me leaving late, or not at all

iOS sometimes reports a departure a few minutes late, and occasionally misses it. After 8 hours the app asks "Still at …?" and offers the real leaving time from your phone's visit log. You can also check out manually from Today, or edit the time — edits are kept in the entry's history so your record stays honest.

Does it drain the battery?

No. LetsLog uses Apple's region and visit monitoring, which run on the phone's low-power location system. It does not track continuously. Typical use adds well under 1% a day.

Where is my data? Can you see it?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and the developer cannot see your data. See the privacy policy.

How do I give my accountant the records?

Report tab → Export. The PDF evidence pack contains hours per week, every visit, mileage, income and costs, receipt images and an integrity check. The CSV export gives the raw rows for spreadsheet or accounting software.

Is the 20-hour figure a rule?

No. HMRC's guidance (CG65715) mentions 20 hours a week as an example of activity indicating a business, and says fewer hours may still qualify. LetsLog shows it as a reference line only. It is not tax advice — please check with your adviser.

Backing up my records, or moving to a new iPhone

Settings → Backup → Save a backup file. It is one file with your whole log, photos, receipts and settings; save it to Files or iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself. On the new iPhone choose Restore from a backup file. The app checks the file is complete before it replaces anything. Backup and restore are free.

I visit several properties in one trip

Each stop is logged from the one before it (within two hours), and the last stop includes the drive home, so the miles add up to the round you actually drove. You can change any trip in its entry.

Quarterly figures for Making Tax Digital

Report shows rent, costs and mileage for each quarterly period, and the CSV export includes a quarterly file with year-to-date totals. LetsLog does not file with HMRC; enter the figures in your Making Tax Digital software or send the file to your accountant.

Restoring a Pro subscription on a new phone

Settings → Restore purchases. Subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID and managed in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Removing sample data

Settings → Sample data → Remove. Sample records are marked "SAMPLE" in any export so they can never be mistaken for real evidence.
