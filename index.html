Questions

Before you ask

Is LetsLog approved by HMRC?

No. LetsLog is an independent record-keeping tool. It is not affiliated with or approved by HMRC, and it is not Making Tax Digital filing software. It produces the records that your accountant or your MTD software needs.

Does it track me all day?

No. It uses Apple's region monitoring to watch only the addresses you add and your home base. It never keeps a continuous track of where you go.

Why does it need location set to "Always"?

So that a visit is logged when the app is closed and the phone is in your pocket. Without it, visits are only recorded while LetsLog is open.

Will it drain my battery?

It uses the phone's low-power location system and does not track continuously, so typical use adds very little.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. There is no account and no LetsLog server, so the developer cannot see it. Route distances and address look-ups go through Apple Maps, as with any Maps app.

What happens if I change phone?

Save a backup file in Settings and restore it on the new iPhone. It holds your whole log, photos, receipts and settings.

Can I use it outside the UK?

LetsLog is built for UK rental properties and UK tax rules, and is offered in the UK App Store only.
